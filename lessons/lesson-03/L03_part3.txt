One more thing worth knowing. Colab can sometimes give you a free graphics card, a GPU, from the Runtime menu. You don't need one yet. Most early labs run perfectly well without it, and free GPU time is limited and changes from day to day. We'll switch one on when deep learning arrives.

A few habits to start now. Name notebooks clearly. Keep datasets in data, and results in outputs. Put the setup cell at the top of every notebook, so you can rerun everything after a restart. And if Colab ever disconnects, don't panic. Reconnect, rerun from the top, and carry on.
