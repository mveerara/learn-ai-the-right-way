===== PART 2: slides 5-8 =====

Every lesson follows the same pattern. You watch a short video. You do a guided lab, which gives you prompts and hints, but never the full code, because you learn by typing it yourself. Then you take a quiz, and the pass mark is eighty percent. And each lesson has its own FAQ, for the questions people ask most.

Now, the entrance quiz. Why have one at all? Because almost every AI tool stands on a few basics. If a lesson says, install the GPU driver with sudo, and sudo is a mystery, you're stuck before the real lesson even starts. The quiz makes sure you have that ground under your feet. It's placement, not a filter. It tells you where to start.

You'll find it in the course catalogue, under Where to start. Press the button that says, take the entrance quiz. It's about twenty easy questions, it takes around fifteen minutes, and it's free. If you learned these skills somewhere else, passing the quiz is all the proof you need.

There are two outcomes. To pass, you need eighty percent overall, with no weak area. Then you register, and go straight to Course one. If you don't pass yet, that's completely fine. The quiz shows exactly which modules you missed. You take only those parts of Course zero, then retake the quiz. Nobody is turned away.
