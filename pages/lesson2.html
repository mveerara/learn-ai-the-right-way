Lesson 2 FAQ

Answers to the questions learners ask most about this lesson.

Do I need to install anything for this lesson?

No. This lesson only explains the plan. You set up step one, Google Colab, in the next lesson, using just a Google account and a browser.

Is Google Colab really free?

Yes, Colab has a free tier that is enough for every lab in the early courses. Its limits, such as how long a session runs or whether a GPU is available, change from time to time, which is one reason your work is saved in GitHub.

What is a repository?

A folder that remembers every change you make to its files. On GitHub it's also backed up online, so you can open it on any computer. Yours will be called workbench.

Do I need a GitHub account now?

Not yet. You'll create a free one in Course 1, Module 2. Until then, Colab saves your work to Google Drive.

My laptop has 4 GB of memory. Can I still follow along?

Yes, for the browser-based courses: Colab runs on Google's computers, not yours. For the laptop lessons, 8 GB is the practical minimum, and 16 GB is comfortable.

Can I use a tablet or a phone?

For watching and for Colab, a tablet works. Typing code on a phone is painful, so use a laptop, desktop or tablet with a keyboard when you can.

What is a dev container?

A box that packs your whole setup, Python version, libraries and tools, so it runs identically on any computer or in the browser with GitHub Codespaces. You'll build one in Course 1, Module 2.

Do I have to pay for Databricks or BigQuery?

No. Databricks has a Free Edition and BigQuery has a sandbox that needs no credit card. You'll only use them in the big data course, and free-tier rules can change, so check their sites then.

Why not just use my laptop for everything?

You can for small and medium data. But some labs need a free GPU (Colab) or many machines at once (Databricks), and companies use all of these, so it pays to know them.

Who is Ria?

Ria is the channel's animated presenter, with an AI-generated voice. The lessons are written by Mukund Veeraraghavan, who designed the course.

Can I ask a question or get help from a mentor?

There's no live support or mentoring. Ask Arjun, our FAQ guide: describe your problem in your own words and he finds the matching answers from every lesson's FAQ. Not answered? Leave a comment on the video; common questions are added to the FAQ.
